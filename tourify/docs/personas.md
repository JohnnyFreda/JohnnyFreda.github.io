# Personas

Who Tourify serves. These guide prioritization. On the **compendium** pillar the indie north
star makes the Dedicated Fan and Music Tourist the sharpest fits; the **social** pillar (see
[vision.md](vision.md)) brings the **Scene Regular** to the center and gives every persona a
reason to open the app between tours.

## Casual Fan
Follows a handful of well-known bands. Wants a simple "is anyone I like playing near me?"
with the occasional nudge. Served well by the MVP + push alerts. Low effort tolerance —
Spotify import matters for this persona.

## Dedicated Fan
Follows many artists, including indie/long-tail acts, and actively hunts tour dates. The
core user the app was born for. Cares most about **coverage** (the north star) and
**timely alerts** so shows don't sell out or slip by.

## Music Tourist
Travels for shows or checks "what's on" in other cities. Values setting/adjusting home
location and radius, and discovering **similar bands** to fill a trip. Benefits from broad
provider coverage.

## Collector / Completionist
Tracks everything and wants to be first to know. Power user of alerts, attendance history,
and (later) social bragging rights ("friends going"). Stress-tests notification
granularity and preferences.

## Scene Regular
Loyal to **rooms and a local scene** more than to any single band — hits the small club down
the street most weekends, knows the regulars, and discovers acts by *who's playing their
venue*. The clearest fit for the **venues-as-first-class** + social pillars: follows venues,
cares about "who among my people follows/attends here," and is the natural early adopter of
connections, stories, and event coordination. Turns Tourify from a tour-tracker into a
**community**, and is the persona that makes the social pillar habitual.
